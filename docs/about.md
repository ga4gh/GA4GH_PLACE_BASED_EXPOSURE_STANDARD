# About

Environmental exposures such as air pollution, diet, physical activity, and social stress
can influence the onset of disease. Environmental data is captured in different ways by
research groups internationally, making it hard to compare or combine.

The [Human Exposome Data Standards Study Group](https://www.ga4gh.org/product/human-exposome-data-standards/)
is developing shared, machine-readable ways to describe environmental exposures. This
schema covers place-based exposures: how a source exposure product was linked to the
locations of cohort participants.

Aims of the group: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21877291>
