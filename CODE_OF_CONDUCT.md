# Code of Conduct

This repository is part of the work of the Global Alliance for Genomics and Health (GA4GH). All contributors and participants, in this repository, in issues and pull requests, and in Work Stream calls and other GA4GH activity, are expected to follow the
**[GA4GH Code of Ethics and Community Conduct (CECC)](https://www.ga4gh.org/code-of-conduct/)**.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behaviour that goes against the CECC, you can:

- Use the [CECC reporting form](https://forms.ga4gh.org/cecc_reporting)
- Email the Safe GA4GH Officer, who offers confidential reporting: [conduct@ga4gh.org](mailto:conduct@ga4gh.org)
- Contact any member of the Safe GA4GH Committee, listed on the [CECC page](https://www.ga4gh.org/code-of-conduct/)

The CECC page also explains how reports are handled.
